Slide 24- 
The slide outlines the theoretical bounds and guarantees provided by a buffer based controller 

Theorem 1: Avoid unnecessary rebuffers 

When the buffer drops near the critical lower threshold(approaches empty), the empty alogrithm enforces Rmin 

As long as the physicall networkk bandwidth is above the bare minimum(Capacity>Rmin) the fill rate exceeds drain rate and the buffer rate is guaranteed to recover/grow 

Theorem 2: Maximise video rate/bandwidth utilisation

When the buffer fills towards its upper safety threshold, the algorithm steps up towards Rmax

As long as capacity<Rmax, chunks take slightly longer to arrive, preventing buffer overflow while fully utilising availabel network throughput
